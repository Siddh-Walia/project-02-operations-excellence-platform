# Business Process Automation & Data Quality

## Enterprise Operations Excellence Platform

### Project Overview

This project explores how business process analysis, data quality controls, operational automation, and management reporting can be integrated into a structured operational platform.

The simulated business scenario involves a fictional international company operating in Japan that receives operational data from multiple departments. The project addresses the challenges of inconsistent data, manual validation, rework, exception handling, and limited operational visibility.

### Business Objectives

- Improve the reliability and consistency of operational data.
- Reduce avoidable manual validation and rework through controlled automation.
- Establish a structured process for identifying, managing, and tracking exceptions.
- Improve operational visibility, accountability, and traceability.
- Provide management with reliable and actionable performance information.
- Enable informed business decisions through clear operational reporting.

### Project Scope

The project will cover:

- Business process analysis and improvement.
- Requirements definition and acceptance criteria.
- Data source analysis and data quality specifications.
- Future-state process and control design.
- System architecture and data modeling.
- Workflow automation and implementation.
- Testing, measurement, and documentation.
- Operational and management reporting.

### Project Approach

The project will be developed through a structured business analysis and solution implementation lifecycle:

1. Project Charter
2. Current-State Process and Root-Cause Analysis
3. Requirements and Acceptance Criteria
4. Data Source, Licensing, Provenance, and Data Quality Specification
5. Future-State Process and Solution Design
6. Architecture and Data Model
7. Implementation, Testing, and Measurement
8. Documentation, Demonstration, and Interview Readiness

### Intended Stakeholders

- Operations Analysts
- Operations Managers and Team Leaders
- Senior Management and Executives
- Data and IT Teams
- Risk and Compliance Stakeholders
- Operational Reporting Users

The primary focus is to enable management to understand operational performance, identify issues, monitor progress, and determine appropriate actions using reliable information.

### Project Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio simulation. The business scenario is fictional. Public datasets may be used as source material, while simulated workflow events and test cases will be clearly distinguished from real source records.

No real-company deployment, business savings, or operational outcomes are claimed.

### Technical Implementation

The implementation will use appropriate technologies for data processing, validation, structured storage, workflow execution, testing, and management reporting. Technology choices will be documented and justified as the project progresses.
